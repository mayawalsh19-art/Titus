## A Toy with a Story

![A colorful toy](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1594787318286-3d835c1d207f?auto=format&fit=crop&w=1200&q=80)

This little toy was found in a box of forgotten treasures. At first, it looked ordinary, but every adventure gave it a new purpose: a brave explorer, a loyal friend, and sometimes the captain of an imaginary spaceship. It never needed batteries or a button to come alive. All it needed was a good story and someone willing to begin it.
